# Business case

RegOps EU is designed as an open-core B2B product concept.

## Why a buyer might care
Security teams already collect SBOMs, CVEs, incident timelines, owners, mitigation plans and legal/compliance input. The operational cost comes from coordinating that evidence under time pressure and proving what happened.

RegOps EU focuses on reducing:
- manual deadline tracking;
- evidence chasing across engineering/security/legal;
- duplicated incident-to-regulatory handoffs;
- unclear accountability;
- inconsistent reporting drafts.

## Monetisation hypothesis
These are product hypotheses, not market-price claims.

- Community / self-hosted: €0
- single-product workspace: €19–29 / month
- startup workspace: €59–79 / month
- team workspace: ~€149 / month
- CRA readiness assessment: €199–499 one-off
- assisted onboarding / evidence mapping: €299–999

A commercial version would add tenant isolation, auth/RBAC, persistent organisations, connectors, scheduled monitoring, collaboration, managed reporting workflows and support.

## Validation strategy
The public portfolio build proves the workflow. A real business would validate willingness-to-pay first through assisted CRA/NIS2 readiness work before investing in a broad SaaS platform.
